# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary website audience is experienced sales professionals from premium finance, wealth management, investment sales, or closely related advisory roles. They are evaluating ETX as a serious next career move and need to understand the role, compensation structure, professional environment, and application process quickly.

Private investors and entrepreneurs remain a secondary audience who need access to ETX's investment-consulting information.

## Product Purpose

The website's primary job is to attract qualified, experienced sales candidates and convert them into a confidential recruiter conversation. It must also preserve a clear secondary path to ETX's investor services.

Success means more qualified Financial Advisor candidates book recruiter calls, while investor information remains findable and credible.

## Positioning

ETX offers experienced premium-finance sales professionals a career path in international investment consulting. The confirmed publishable compensation claim for the Financial Advisor role is a $1,500 base plus KPI bonuses.

## Operating Context

The site is bilingual in Russian and English. Candidates may arrive from referrals, search, direct links, or HH.ru and commonly evaluate the employer on mobile before deciding whether to speak with a recruiter.

The preferred primary application action is booking a confidential recruiter call. HH.ru remains a secondary application route.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Recruitment is the primary website goal.
- The Financial Advisor role is the flagship sales opportunity.
- Compensation may be described only as "$1,500 base + KPI bonuses"; total earnings ranges and commission formulas are not approved for publication.
- Investor-service pages and legal risk language must remain available.
- The existing React, Vite, Tailwind, and Russian/English localization architecture remains in place.
- Claims about lead volume, employee performance, promotion timing, named leadership, regulatory status, or testimonials must not be invented.
- Recruiter-specific contact details and genuine team or office media remain open content decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the ETX name and the existing restrained, professional dark-navy and gold identity. The voice should feel credible, direct, ambitious, and appropriate for experienced financial-sales professionals without hype or guaranteed-income language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved compensation statement: $1,500 base plus KPI bonuses.
- Existing bilingual role descriptions, selection stages, company address, and investor-service information in the repository.
- No approved employee testimonials, performance benchmarks, total-earnings examples, named leadership profiles, or genuine team/office photography are available in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Experienced professionals first: speak to proven judgment and sales ability, not entry-level ambition.
- Specific over promotional: use approved facts and make unknowns explicit.
- Confidential, low-friction conversion: the recruiter conversation is the primary action.
- Employer credibility before application: establish role expectations, compensation, process, and company context before asking for details.
- Two audiences, one clear hierarchy: recruitment leads; investor services remain accessible without competing with the candidate journey.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain keyboard accessible, responsive, compatible with reduced-motion preferences, and usable with international phone numbers.
